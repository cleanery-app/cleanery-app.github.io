What the app does

Cleanery helps you find photos and videos you may not need and delete them. It suggests; you decide. Nothing is deleted until you confirm in the iOS dialog, and deleted items stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Before deleting, check what you selected. We aren't responsible for photos you chose to delete.

Free use

Scanning, browsing and marking photos are free. Your first cleanup is free too: one deletion of any size, with no time limit. It's used up once a deletion goes through.
